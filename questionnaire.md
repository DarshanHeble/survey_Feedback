**Purpose of the Questionnaire:**  
Dear Participant,  
You are invited to participate in this research study that aims to understand students' ability to distinguish between AI-generated and human-written source code. The questionnaire consists of programming code snippets written in different programming languages. Your task is to identify whether you think each snippet was written by a human or generated using Artificial Intelligence. Your participation is voluntary, and all responses will remain anonymous and be used solely for academic research purposes.
